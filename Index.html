Our Story

A kitchen built on grandmother's recipes

Muthazham started as a family kitchen before it was a restaurant. Every dish is made fresh to order — mustard seeds tempered in coconut oil, fish sourced daily, and nothing rushed.

We are a small, passionate team from Payyanur, bringing you the taste of Malabar.
